# 4Nguru The Brand Online

South African clothing brand selling online, priced in Rand. Customers order through the shop or on WhatsApp, and delivery covers South Africa. The site also runs events with QR tickets and a community page with a gallery and mailing list.

## Who uses it

Shoppers in South Africa, mostly arriving on phones from social links and WhatsApp, browsing by category or by what is new. The owner runs orders, stock and events from the admin screens.

## Register

Brand storefront. The clothes and their photographs are the content; the interface should recede around them while staying plainly a shop: visible prices, stock state and a short path to the cart or WhatsApp.

## Brand commitments

- The multicolour 4-N-g-u-r-u wordmark stays exactly as it is until a real logo is supplied.
- WhatsApp is a first-class way to order, not a fallback.
- Only claims the owner has confirmed appear on the site. No invented reviews, numbers or promises.

## Constraints

- Server rendered React Router app; the look is token-driven through `data-theme`, set at build time by `VITE_THEME`.
- The owner reviews before anything reaches `main`.
- Honour reduced motion; body text at 4.5:1 or better.
