Scope: 4Nguru storefront, gallery theme (VITE_THEME=gallery). Home is Persuade; shop, product, cart and checkout are Operate inside the same world.
Audience: South African shoppers, mostly on phones, from social links and WhatsApp.
Pinned by the user: cosmos.so as the inspiration, look and layout scope, headline and search as the home opener.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shop as a quiet image gallery you search. It refuses the banner-plus-card-row clothing template: the first screen is one sentence and one working search.

OWN-WORLD: White ground fading from a soft neutral grey, near-black ink, Schibsted Grotesk at tight tracking, full pills for every control, nested-pill search, soft rounded image tiles with no card chrome. The only colour comes from the wordmark and the product photographs.

STORY: The visitor sees what the shop is, searches or taps Shop all, scans large photo tiles with name and price, then browses categories as a large dimmed list that lights up on hover.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centred two-line headline, "Find your / next fit", followed by a small overlapping stack of real product photos when photos exist, at up to 88px. Under it the search pill at full width to 640px, whose placeholder steps through the category names once, then Shop all and Ask on WhatsApp pills, then the delivery line.

FORM: Brief-pinned direction from cosmos.so, so no roll was made. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
